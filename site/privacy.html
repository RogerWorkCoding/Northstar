Northstar Privacy

Last updated October 14, 2026 · Draft for testers

The short version

  • Northstar remembers only what you tell it or confirm. You can see, change or delete all of it.
  • It never saves your feelings or health unless you ask it to.
  • No ads. We never sell your information.
  • You can turn memory off, or delete your whole account, in Settings.

What Northstar keeps

WhatWhyWhen

Northstar does not use your location, contacts or photos unless you share something with it yourself.

Who helps run Northstar

To work, Northstar sends some information to a few companies. They handle it for us under their terms for paying developers.

  • Anthropic (Claude) writes Northstar's replies and does web searches. Your message and the memory Northstar needs for that reply are sent with it.
  • Google (Veo) makes videos from the shot prompt you choose.
  • Supabase stores your account, memory, chats and videos securely.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, maps and test versions of the app.
  • YouTube plays lesson videos when you open one.

Your choices

  • See it: Settings → Review memory, and Search.
  • Take it with you: Settings → Export my memory.
  • Delete memory: Settings → Delete all memory.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete my account. This removes your account, memory, notes, chats and videos for good.
  • Turn memory off: Settings → Memory. Nothing new is saved while it's off.

Hard moments

Northstar is not a therapist, doctor or lawyer. If you share something that sounds like a crisis, it points you to real help first, such as the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline in the US. What you share in those moments isn't saved unless you ask.

Children

Northstar is made for adults. It isn't meant for anyone under 18.
